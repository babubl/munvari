Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

MunVari is a free advance tax calculator for India, available as a website and an Android app. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

The short version

MunVari does not collect, store or send your personal or financial information anywhere. Every calculation runs on your own device.

What you enter

Income, deductions, TDS and tax payments you type into MunVari stay on your device. They are never uploaded to a server, because MunVari has no server that receives them.

If you tick Remember on this device, your entries are saved in your browser's or app's local storage so they are there next time. Untick it, or use Clear all, to delete them. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data also removes them.

What MunVari does not do

  • No account or login
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising code
  • No cookies
  • No third-party fonts or scripts; everything is served from MunVari itself
  • No sale or sharing of data, because no data is collected

Small settings kept on your device

MunVari remembers your choice of light or dark mode and text size in local storage. These settings contain no personal information.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other services in your browser: the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal to pay tax, and, in the Android app, Google Calendar to save a reminder. Those services have their own privacy policies. MunVari only passes the reminder title and date you see on screen.

Children

MunVari is meant for adult taxpayers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
