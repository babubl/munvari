What MunVari is

MunVari is a free educational estimator that helps Indian taxpayers work out advance tax installments and interest under Sections 234B and 234C for FY 2026-27, using Budget 2026 rates under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Not tax advice

MunVari's results are estimates based on the information you provide and on simplifications described in the calculator's notes. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. Before paying tax or filing a return, verify the figures on the Income Tax Department's e-filing portal or with a qualified tax professional.
