Law and section numbers

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the Income-tax Act, 1961. MunVari shows the new section with the old one in brackets.

TopicIncome-tax Act, 2025Old (1961)
Liability to pay advance taxSec 404Sec 208
Interest for not paying enough advance taxSec 424Sec 234B
Interest for late or short installmentsSec 425Sec 234C
Rebate for resident individualsSec 156Sec 87A
Presumptive income (business, profession)Sec 58Sec 44AD / 44ADA

Deduction and capital-gains labels (80C, 80D, 111A, 112A and similar) still use the 1961 numbers, which the e-filing portal and AIS continue to show. They will be switched once the official correspondence table is published.

Known simplifications

  • The employer NPS cap is applied to gross salary as entered; the law applies it to basic pay plus dearness allowance.
  • For surcharge marginal relief, income above the threshold is taken off slab income first, then 111A, 112 and 112A gains.
  • The share of tax on dividends, for the 15% surcharge cap, is taken in proportion to slab income.
  • Treaty (DTAA) rates for non-residents, agricultural income, companies and trusts are not covered.
