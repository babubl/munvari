▶Profilebasics

Non-residents: most Indian income already has TDS at higher rates, and a tax treaty (DTAA) may lower the tax. MunVari doesn't apply treaty rates — check with a CA if you claim treaty benefits.

▶Salary
Allowed up to 14% of salary (new regime) or 10% (old). MunVari applies the cap to gross salary; the law uses basic + DA.
▶Interest income
Old regime: 80TTA ₹10K / 80TTB ₹50K (seniors) applied automatically
▶Rental income
Old regime: loss set-off capped at ₹2L. New regime: no set-off against other income.
▶Capital gains

Pick the quarter each gain was / will be realised — this drives the Section 234C relief.

Debt mutual funds bought on or after 1 April 2023 are taxed at slab rate however long you hold them — enter those gains as STCG on other assets.

Capital losses

Short-term losses can reduce any capital gain; long-term losses only long-term gains. Brought-forward losses must come from returns filed on time (up to 8 years).

▶Dividends
Companies deduct 10% TDS if dividend exceeds ₹10,000/year
▶Business / profession

F&O or intraday trading? Choose Regular books and enter net profit. F&O is non-speculative business income; intraday equity is speculative. Audit and loss rules apply — see a CA if your turnover is large.
